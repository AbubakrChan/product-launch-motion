## Register map

Two grounds, alternating by act. Every register change is a story turn and carries a
transition.

- **Argument register** (the problem and the thesis): e.g. dark `#100E19`
- **Product register** (the demo and the evidence): e.g. light `#FAFAFB`
- **Accent**: one colour or gradient
- **Accent budget** (where it may appear — five jobs maximum):
  1. one emphasis word per headline
  2. stat numerals
  3. the active step in a sequence
  4. state flips
  5. the CTA
